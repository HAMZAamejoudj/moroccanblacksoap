# External SEO (backlinks)

On-page fixes live in the codebase. **External factors** in audit tools measure **links from other websites** — they cannot reach 95%+ from code alone.

## Quick wins (do these on your accounts)

1. **Google Search Console** — verify `www.moroccanblacksoap.com`, submit `https://www.moroccanblacksoap.com/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Social profiles** — add the live site URL in Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok bios (same URLs as `ORGANIZATION.sameAs` in `src/lib/site.ts`).
3. **Google Business Profile** — Marrakesh address + website link if you serve B2B locally.
4. **Partner / supplier listings** — Moroccan export directories, beauty wholesale catalogs, ECOCERT or coop partner pages where allowed.

## Link building (medium term)

- Guest posts or supplier features on spa / hammam / natural beauty blogs (anchor: “Moroccan black soap wholesale”).
- Press / “As seen in” only when you have real citations.
- Avoid paid link farms — they hurt more than they help.

Re-run your SEO checker on `https://www.moroccanblacksoap.com/en` after each deploy.
